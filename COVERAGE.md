# Nashua coverage audit - updated October 3, 2026

Expanded from 226 to 282 source-linked listings. The latest pass adds 16 to the previous 266. This is citywide geographic coverage, not proof that every currently operating restaurant has been captured. Counts include 3 closed historical listings, 1 temporary closure and 9 unresolved listings. The 269 records marked listed have not all been verified as currently operating. Fields without reviewed evidence remain unknown. The number of missing current businesses is unknown.

## Evidence and census gaps

The original 226-record baseline is preserved in research/before-expansion.json. An expanded OSM query inspected 229 records within Nashua's administrative boundary, including restaurants, cafes, fast food, ice cream, pubs, bars and bakery/coffee/deli shops. Its underlying database timestamp is 2026-07-15T15:22:01Z; the October 2 retrieval does not make those business tags current. Each new record includes reviewed source URLs and address geometry evidence.

The [city food-service page](https://www.nashuanh.gov/525/Food-Service-Safety) did not provide a downloadable restaurant roster. Exhaustive Google Maps, Yelp and Tripadvisor collection remains unavailable. To establish completeness, reconcile an authorized full provider export or city licensed-facility roster against this directory, resolve older names and closures, and verify every unmatched current business. Broad geographic coverage alone cannot establish a complete census.

Private institutional cafeterias, grocery stores without a verified prepared-food counter, candy retail, delivery-only aliases and unconfirmed proposed openings are not added as restaurants. Restaurants inside malls, hotels and casinos are included when specifically identified. Casino access restrictions are recorded where known. Unknown hours, prices, ratings and signature dishes are not filled with estimates.
