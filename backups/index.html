How to read the listings

Ratings are shown with their provider and capture date. A directory listing does not confirm a business is still open. Temporary closures and conflicting predecessor names are marked. Approximate pins are labeled; use the linked address for directions.

Open-now uses published weekly hours in America/New_York. Holiday changes are not included. Unknown or conflicting hours are excluded from the open-now filter.

Google Maps, Yelp and Tripadvisor links let you cross-check each place. A search link is not a verified profile. Some provider pages could not be accessed, so this app does not claim full coverage of those platforms.

“Step inside” opens Google’s panorama explorer near the venue. Interior tours and photo spheres are only available where contributed; an interior is not guaranteed.

The dashed lime outline follows the City of Nashua GIS city boundary; surrounding towns are dimmed. All 282 current directory pins fall inside that boundary. This checks mapped coordinates, not whether each business is still operating.
